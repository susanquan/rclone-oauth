Privacy Policy

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner to run rclone against their own Google Drive account.

  • No data is collected, stored, or shared by this application's owner or any third party.
  • Files and credentials accessed through Google APIs stay on the owner's own computer, where rclone runs.
  • Google user data is used only to perform the file operations the owner explicitly requests.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
